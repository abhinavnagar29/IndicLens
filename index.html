How well do LLMs understand
Indian culture?

IndicLens is an evaluation benchmark designed to measure cultural alignment in large language models across five dimensions: cultural knowledge, social bias, legal and governance understanding, multilingual comprehension, and code-switching. The goal is to go beyond language proficiency and ask whether a model actually understands India.
